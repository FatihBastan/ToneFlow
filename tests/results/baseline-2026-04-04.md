## Primary Failure: Harmonic Octave Confusion

The NSDF picks the *highest* correlation peak instead of the *first* significant peak.
With harmonic-rich timbres, a harmonic period often has higher correlation than the fundamental.

### Error Distribution
- ~60% of failures: detected 1 octave too low
- ~25% of failures: detected 2+ octaves too low  
- ~15% of failures: locked onto sub-harmonic (wrong note entirely)

### What works perfectly
- All math/mapping functions: 100%
- Pure sine wave detection: ~100%
- Silence/noise rejection: 100%
- Streak tier logic: 100%
- Low-frequency detection (E2, A2 range): good

### What fails
- Any instrument with strong harmonics above ~150 Hz
- Bowed strings (sawtooth harmonics) worst affected
- Vocals with formant harmonics severely affected
- Guitar harmonics cause consistent octave-down errors
