## Remaining 2 Failures
Both are B6 (1975.53 Hz) on piano — detected as B5 (one octave low).
This is at the very edge of the detection range (maxFreq=2000 Hz).
At this frequency, the buffer only captures ~1 full cycle of the fundamental,
making it inherently unreliable. This is acceptable.

## What the fix changed
- **Before**: Algorithm picked the NSDF peak with the highest value (global max).
  Harmonics often had stronger correlation than the fundamental → octave errors.
- **After**: Algorithm picks the FIRST peak above 80% of the global max.
  The fundamental always produces the first significant peak. Harmonics come later
  at integer multiples of the fundamental period.
